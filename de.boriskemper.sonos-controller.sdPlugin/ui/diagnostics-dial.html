Speaker not showing up? If your speaker isn't showing up in the list (e.g. it's on a different VLAN), enter the IP address of any one reachable Sonos speaker here. This only needs to be done once, in a single spot — every speaker in your household will then be found everywhere in the plugin. Afterwards, still pick your device from the dropdown above as usual.
Rotate or press the dial to scroll through metrics, tap to refresh now. Latency — response time of the diagnostic query, a rough stand-in for how responsive the speaker's WiFi link is right now. Consistently high or spiky values: move the speaker closer to the router/access point, reduce interference (other 2.4/5 GHz devices, microwaves), or check for a firmware update.

PHY Errors — radio-layer errors counted since the last poll. The occasional low count is normal; a clearly rising trend points to interference or a weak signal — same fixes as above.

Noise Floor — background radio noise in dBm. More negative is quieter/better (roughly -90 to -110 is healthy); values near 0 are unusually loud, or mean the radio simply hasn't measured yet. If it stays near 0 or keeps climbing, look for a nearby interference source, or consider a wired connection for that speaker.
